Build street cred: rob stores, steal cars and sell them at the chop shop. Crimes raise your wanted level. Break line of sight with the police to escape, or get busted.

Your crew: homies hang out by the park where you start (G on the radar). Walk up and press E / D-pad ↑ to recruit up to 3. Each has their own heart: some run, some take cover, some charge. They ride in your car and fight with you.

Gunfight test: the Southside Kings hold the corner marked purple on the minimap (Southgate). Walk up or open fire and they shoot back. Police start shooting at 3 stars or if you shoot at them.
